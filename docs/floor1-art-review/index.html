Stairway Down

(20,2) · stairs panel hero asset
Full architectural wall-face replacement via the new per-theme LoadedTileset.stairs slot (same substitution point as door.png). Reads as a real descending stairwell at 1/2/4-tile approach; at distance it glows as a strong landmark against the flanking ember-suture (f3) zone. Polish pass: the bottom ~10% of the panel is now alpha-feathered (scripts/feather-stairs.mjs) so the live floor-cast shows through at the seam instead of a hard static edge — measurably better at 1-tile range. At 2+ tiles it still reads partly as "a picture in a frame," but that's mostly the pre-existing flanking ivy billboards, not this texture; not fully closed. Minor known issue: a faint centerline seam appears only when the camera is perfectly axis-aligned — a pre-existing raycaster tie-break, not unique to this asset (documented, not fixed this pass).

"THE THIRD BELL HAS NO TONGUE." Top-anchored in the cut-bell chapel niche, rope cut at the frame edge. One of the strongest results — clean silhouette, matches the f4 choir palette precisely.

Cold-Hand Shrine

(7,6) e · wallFeature decal
"A cold hand closes your wounds" (heal event). Carved stone hand reaching from a niche. Eerie, well-matched to the chapel stone, no gore/cartoon-hand failure modes. Polish pass: fully redone as an arched shrine niche with the wrist disappearing into the recess and a small reliquary ledge below — the original read as a hand mounted on the wall (mitten silhouette at gameplay scale); this now reads unmistakably as a hand emerging from the wall.

Sweating Iron Plate

(18,6) e · wallFeature decal
"Iron sweats. Something below coughs once." Riveted plate with glowing ember cracks at the base, bolted into the ember-suture wall. Strongest of the six decals — forge infrastructure that genuinely looks like it doesn't belong in a crypt. Polish pass: distressed the surrounding masonry so it chips into the plate's edge (two earlier attempts overcorrected into losing the metal-plate identity entirely, rejected before landing here) while keeping the four corner rivets and glowing seams unmistakably metal.
