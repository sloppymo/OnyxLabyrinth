## Transitional implementation facts and stale claims

The facts below help diagnose the currently shipped legacy game. They do not override the new product direction. In particular, random-encounter, gear-economy, perk, classic-combat, and wipe-to-Town facts are removal/migration constraints—not target-design requirements.

- ~~"The campaign roster is six/four selectable characters"~~ — **false for the current product.** New campaigns use the fixed Old Man + Rat King duo. `Character[]` / `GameState.party` and `LEGACY_PARTY_SIZE` remain only for combat/save compatibility and old-save normalization. Do not resurrect the retired creation or roster-selection flow.
- **Boss formations are ordinary weight-1 entries in the random encounter tables on floors 3-5** (`data/enemies.ts` — `headmasters-echo`/`-remnant`/`-ascendant` at lines ~1447/1549/1663). This is not stale, it is just non-obvious, and it invalidates a whole family of designs: **any mechanic that filters or rerolls the encounter table shifts boss odds.** Measured 2026-08-01 — suppressing the below-median half of each table (a Pokémon-Repel-style "ward" spell) raised the boss entry's conditional share **×2.38 / ×1.85 / ×2.17** on F3/F4/F5, turning an encounter-avoidance spell into a boss-fishing tool. Excluding bosses from the filter makes it *worse*, since the boss becomes the one entry guaranteed to survive. Prefer a flat rate scalar over table filtering, or add explicit per-entry ward/rarity metadata first.
- **The pity ramp, not the base rate, sets encounter spacing.** With the force at step 28, expected steps between fights is ~13-16 at shipped rates; reducing the base rate to **zero** only raises that to ~23. So any "fewer encounters" item/spell built on a rate multiplier alone buys ~4 steps and is close to a placebo verb — the binding constraint is `ENCOUNTER_PITY_FORCE`. Do not re-derive this from scratch.

- ~~Headmaster / academy as campaign lore~~ — superseded 2026-07-25 by `superpowers/specs/2026-07-25-labyrinth-narrative-design.md` (gods left, Death left, lamp/djinn, the kept).
