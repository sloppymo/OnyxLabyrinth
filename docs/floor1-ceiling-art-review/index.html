Floor 1 — Ceiling Environment Art Pass

Two new renderer primitives — ceilingSprites (top-anchored hanging billboards) and ceilingFeatures (per-cell ceiling texture overrides) — plus an 18-asset PixelLab batch, 15 of which are placed into Floor 1's five regional zones. All screenshots captured live in the production raycaster (vite preview), not isolated PNGs. Branch agent/ceiling-environment-art.

Baseline — for comparison

Plain ceiling, no art (Floor 2)

reference
Floor 2 has zero ceiling art. Included because a straight corridor's own base ceiling texture already shows a radiating beam/plank pattern under this engine's affine perspective cast — worth seeing once so it isn't mistaken for a rendering bug in the cards below (it was, briefly, during this pass's own QA).

Hero landmark — full 1 / 2 / 4-tile + off-axis

Hanging Iron Cage ACCEPT

(11,18) · ceiling sprite · id f1-cage · crypt spine
Three chains converge to a real attachment point at the ceiling line at every distance tested — the top never freezes or slides as the party approaches, which was the one thing this whole primitive had to get right. At 1 tile it fills most of the screen (deliberately unclamped, like floor decor); at 4 tiles it's a small, clean landmark silhouette. The off-axis shot (bottom image, standing in a side room looking through a doorway) shows correct wall occlusion: the wall corner clips most of the cage, a small sliver shows through the open doorway, no bleed-through. Reads dark against a dark unlit corridor — consistent with how the existing wallFeature decals read in the same lighting, not a defect unique to this asset.

Chapel zone (theme f4, NW)

Chapel Relief + Censer (combined view) ACCEPT

(6,4) feature · (7,4) sprite · ceiling feature ceiling sprite
The best single shot in the pass: the relief medallion (f1-ceiling-namanda-mark) fills the near ceiling exactly where the per-cell UV mapping says it should, and the censer (f1-censer) is correctly visible two tiles further down the same room, both legible in one frame. No canonical Namanda symbol exists anywhere in the merged codebase (checked — see the generation log), so the relief is a neutral worn sun/wheel carving, not invented iconography; the censer is the task's suggested Namanda-compatible fallback asset.

Hanging Censer, close ACCEPT

(7,4) · ceiling sprite · id f1-censer
Standing directly under it: chains attach cleanly at the ceiling, bowl and lid detail hold up at full scale, good contrast against the lighter chapel stone (the chapel's palette reads noticeably better than the unlit crypt corridors — worth reusing this framing for future landmark placements).

Cracked Hanging Bell ACCEPT

(4,6) · ceiling sprite · id f1-bell-cracked
Clearly distinct from the existing wall-mounted tongueless bell wallFeature two cells away — this one unmistakably hangs (bracket + drop), the other is set into the wall face. Reads well at 2 tiles.

Forge / ember-suture zone (theme f3, NE)

Chain + Hook, paired with Chain Plate ACCEPT

(19,3) both · ceiling sprite ceiling feature · near the stairs landmark
A ceiling feature and a ceiling sprite placed on the same cell on purpose: the riveted attachment plate (f1-ceiling-chain-plate) sits directly above the hook chain (f1-chain-hook), so the chain visibly attaches to hardware instead of just disappearing into plain stone — the strongest demonstration in this pass of "the top attachment is important." Good ember-orange forge palette against the scorched wall.

Forge Chain Counterweight ACCEPT

(13,4) · ceiling sprite · id f1-forge-counterweight
Reroll (v2) — the first candidate read as a second lantern (windowed, glowing). This version is a solid opaque iron mass with visible rivets, clearly a weight rather than a light source. Distinct silhouette from every other hanging asset in the set.

Unfinished-index / library zone (theme f2, SW)

Hanging Lantern ACCEPT WITH NOTES

(7,14) · ceiling sprite · id f1-lantern-hanging
Found and fixed during QA: the first placement (7,12) landed on the exact same cell as a pre-existing floor-standing crate mapSprite, and the two billboards overlapped into what looked like a broken opaque orange rectangle — a placement bug, not a render bug (confirmed by inspecting the source PNG's alpha channel directly: clean 0/255, no partial values). Moved to (7,14), a clean cell nearby; the fix is a reminder to cross-check new placements against existing mapSprites/wallFeatures/events/treasures coordinates, which this pass now does for all 15 final placements.

Broken Masonry Hole ACCEPT

(3,16) · ceiling feature · id f1-ceiling-hole
Reads as damage/decay fitting the "unfinished" library theme — a gap in the wooden-beamed ceiling revealing rougher stone above. A treasure chest is visible in the same shot purely by coincidence of the corridor layout; good incidental composition.

Upward-cistern zone (theme f5, SE)

Ceiling Drain + Root Curtain ACCEPT

(17,15) feature · (17,13) sprite · ceiling feature ceiling sprite
The drain (f1-ceiling-drain) sits directly on the same cell as the existing "black water drips upward" event and the upward-water wallFeature — the water now has a visible source overhead, not just a wall decal. Root curtain (f1-root-curtain) at 1 tile (right image) is the most dramatic single frame in the set: irregular strand lengths, solid mass at the ceiling line, genuine sense of things hanging down into the party's space. Cistern's teal fog tint does a lot of work for mood here.

Long Rusted Chain ACCEPT WITH NOTES

(21,16) · ceiling sprite · id f1-chain-long
Found and fixed during QA: ceiling sprites were originally drawn into a forced square region (size × size), copied from drawMapSprites without noticing that mapSprite art is uniformly 32×32 while this pass's art is intentionally tall-and-narrow (a 56×144 chain vs. an 88×128 cage). Squares distorted each asset by a different, art-dependent amount. Fixed in renderer.ts: width is now derived from each image's own aspect ratio, with size driving height. This screenshot is post-fix — the chain reads as genuinely thin, matching the source art, not stretched.

Water-Damaged Ceiling Patch ACCEPT WITH NOTES

(21,13) · ceiling feature · id f1-ceiling-water-stain
This is the deliberately subtle "ordinary dressing" entry the task brief asked for, and at 1 tile in a dim corridor it is very subtle — close to invisible without knowing to look for it. That matches the brief ("subtle not overwhelming") but is worth flagging: if a future pass wants players to actually register this as authored content rather than texture noise, it needs more contrast. Shipping as-is since under-stating an "ordinary" tile is the safer failure mode.

Crypt spine (theme f1, central corridor)

Short Chain Pair ACCEPT

(11,24) · ceiling sprite · id f1-chain-broken
Reads clearly as two hanging chains at distance. Less "asymmetric/snapped" than the brief asked for — the first candidate's two chains are similar length, and a reroll aimed at a single obviously snapped chain came back visually noisy and was rejected (kept on disk, not shipped). Serviceable generic crypt dressing rather than a distinct story beat; a good candidate to revisit with a third attempt in a future pass.

Wooden Cross-Beam ACCEPT

(11,15) · ceiling feature · id f1-ceiling-beam
X-brace timber panel with iron corner fittings, clearly distinct from the surrounding plain crypt ceiling's cracked-stone pattern. The red mark visible in the background is an unrelated, pre-existing locked door (crypt-key) four tiles further down the same corridor — flagging it explicitly because it was mistaken for part of this asset during the first look at this screenshot.
