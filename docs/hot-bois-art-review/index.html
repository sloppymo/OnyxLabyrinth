Hot Boi's Tavern — Interior Art (P0 + P1)

Branch agent/hot-bois-interior-art. Core surfaces, the three-part bar, Hot Boi's proprietor billboard, the central pillar (P0) — plus the hearth, chandelier, hanging bar rack, core furniture, varied wall art, and kitchen core (P1). Every in-engine screenshot here is the real renderer (vite preview), reached by temporarily wiring each asset onto a real Floor 1 wall/cell, screenshotting, then reverting the floor data — only the art files and registry entries are committed.

P0 + P1 both complete. P2 (secondary lantern, clutter compositions, kitchen/bar/table clutter, optional material variants) not started — the brief's own stop conditions say to stop once "additional generations start becoming redundant," and the room now has enough furnishing options to compose from.

A. Core Materials

256×256, tileable, RGB. The floor checkerboard alternates floorA/floorB the same way the campaign tilesets do.

B. The Bar

Geometry decision: the floorplan's north row is read as the north wall face of the row-1 cells — one near-full-face wallFeature decal per cell (widthFrac 0.98 / heightFrac 0.95 / anchor bottom, verified empirically before generating any art), counter + back-bar shelving baked into a single composition each. Hot Boi is a separate NPC billboard, not baked into the wall art.

C. Central Pillar

visual-only No 4-sided solid-interior-cell primitive exists in this renderer (walls are per-edge, not per-cell-fill) — building one is out of scope for an art pass. This ships as a tall billboard; real obstruction is a follow-up engine task, not solved here.

E. Hearth

One of the top-3 landmark compositions per the brief (bar+Hot Boi, pillar, hearth). Same near-full-face wallFeature treatment as the bar.

F. Chandelier & Hanging Bar Rack

Ceiling sprites. The chandelier's baseSize (56) is pushed well above every other ceiling sprite in the game — deliberately, per the "big fake geometry" principle from the ceiling-art pass.

G. Furniture

Curated per the brief's own "1-2 compositions rather than dozens of props" guidance — table and keg-stack are baked scenes (mugs+bread; barrels+tapped keg), not bare objects. Bar stools live in hotboi-bar-center; standalone crate/barrel reuse the base game's generic props.

H. Wall Art — Anti-Slop Check

F1's wall-decal batch drew a "C" for centered-rectangle repetition. These three are deliberately not that: an irregular collage (bottom-anchored), a trophy mounted high (top-anchored), a narrow rack at eye height (center-anchored, 18% width).

Notice-board took 4 candidates — v1-v3 all collapsed to a blank rectangular panel despite explicit "no frame/no board" language; describing it as "four separate rotated paper scraps" instead of "a board" is what finally broke the model's prior. Full history in the generation log.

I. Kitchen (core)

Minimum P1 kitchen set. Clutter compositions (cookware rack, cauldron, sack piles) are P2, not generated here.

J. Composition Board

The floorplan is canonical (from the task brief text — no floorplan image file was supplied, so this is a schematic, not a redesign). Not true first-person; the point is proving every asset belongs to one room.
